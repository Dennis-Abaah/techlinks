Invoicing, Payment Terms & Overdue Accounts

TechLinks adheres to transparent and structured milestone-based billing to ensure security and predictability for both parties.

1. Deposit Requirement: Unless otherwise specified in a formal contract, a non-refundable upfront commitment deposit of 50% of the total project value is required before project discovery, wireframing, or development initiates.

2. Milestone Disbursements: Multi-phase development projects (e.g., full-stack software or mobile apps) may follow a 40% initial / 30% alpha staging / 30% pre-launch release structure as outlined in the SOW.

3. Final Settlement: The remaining balance of the project fee must be paid in full prior to the release of source code repositories, migration to production servers, live domain point-over, or submission of apps to the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

4. Accepted Payment Channels: Payments may be remitted via Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money), direct Bank Wire Transfer, or authorized online payment gateways (such as Paystack, Stripe, or Flutterwave).

5. Late Payments & Project Halts: Invoices not settled within seven (7) business days of issuance shall accrue late interest at 2.5% per month or the maximum rate permissible by law. TechLinks reserves the right to halt active work, withhold staging server access, or temporarily unpublish live assets until overdue balances are fully settled.
